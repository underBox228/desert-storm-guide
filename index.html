50M+ · 1-2 players

⚠️ Fallback Strategy — When Enemy Is Stronger

Don't overextend against a stronger opponent. Consolidate on three buildings your team can actually defend:

Info Center~pts/min + Oil Refinery 13000 pts/min + Oil Refinery 23000 pts/min

That combination generates 6000+ pts/min — more than an enemy holding only the Nuclear Silo (4500 pts/min). You don't need the Silo to win. A disciplined, coordinated defense of these three buildings is a legitimate victory path.

The moment the enemy spreads thin to chase you off hospitals or the Science Hub, their Silo becomes vulnerable. Patience wins.

General Rules

🚫
NO players below 50M near the Nuclear Silo — ever The Silo is contested by the strongest enemies on the map. Sending underpowered players there wastes them and weakens your Silo defense. Below 50M? Stay away.
🤝
Play for the team, not for personal points Chasing individual score while the Oil Refinery falls costs the whole alliance the match. Prioritize map control and building defense over kill counts or personal honor points.
R-01
No <50M squads on the wall — squads under 50M power must stay off the walls entirely. Do not place any squad on a wall position.
R-02
Attack with your 1st squad only. When capturing or defending a building, use squad 1 exclusively. Squads 2 and 3 are weaker — sending them into a fight means losing soldiers for nothing.
R-03
2nd/3rd squad: empty walls only. Use your 2nd or 3rd squad to capture undefended buildings only. Scout first. Track whether the enemy's 1st squad (the one with the monkey) is out of base before engaging.
R-04
Only attack stronger enemies when their main squad is out. If enemy is stronger, do not engage unless their 1st squad has left base. Wait for the right moment.
R-05
Don't teleport to already-held buildings. If a teammate is holding a building and no enemy is nearby — find another target. You are wasted there. Check the map before teleporting.
R-06
No soldiers? Still fight. Zero troops does not mean you are done. Teleport to empty buildings and capture them — even one player can flip an undefended point.
R-07
55M+ and 50M+: never teleport to hospitals. Your power is too valuable. Let light forces handle hospitals — your role is Silo, Refineries, and Info Center.
R-08
Outmatched at a building? Pull back cleanly. If a stronger enemy approaches and you cannot hold — remove squads from walls, save your troops, and let the building go. Don't die for a lost cause.

Extra Tips

  • Grab supply boxes immediately. When a building changes hands, a supply box drops nearby. These give instant bonus points. The first player there claims them — don't ignore them.
  • Reinforce, don't stack. If a teammate is holding a building safely, don't pile in — move to the next uncaptured target. Two strong players on one building means another is undefended.
  • Track the enemy's 1st squad (monkey marker). Knowing where their strongest player is at all times tells you which buildings are vulnerable and when to strike.
